# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- "Cohorts" in the reporting menu: the visitors who came for the first time in the same day, week or month, and how many of them came back or converted a goal in the periods after.
- The page shows it as how many of 100 new visitors came back, as a chart of all cohorts with the cohort you pick next to it, and as a table of every cohort.
- The page tells when its report was generated.
- The API method `RebelCohorts.getCohorts`.
